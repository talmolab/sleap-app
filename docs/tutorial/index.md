# Tutorial

This tutorial walks through the whole SLEAP workflow on a sample video of two
mice: label a frame, train a model, correct its predictions, retrain, then track a
new video and export the results. Each page is one stage.

1. [Setup](setup.md)
2. [Create a project](new-project.md)
3. [Initial labeling](initial-labeling.md)
4. [Training a model](training.md)
5. [Correcting predictions](correcting-predictions.md)
6. [Tracking a new video](tracking.md)
7. [Proofreading](proofreading.md)
8. [Exporting the results](exporting.md)
9. [What's next](next-steps.md)

!!! tip "Prefer to be shown?"

    Click **Start Tutorial** in the app's menu bar. It walks through steps 1–5
    in the app itself, highlighting each control, and moves on once you've done
    each step, then runs your model on the whole training video. In the browser it stops after labeling.
