Tutorial¶

This tutorial walks through the whole SLEAP workflow on a sample video of two mice: label a frame, train a model, correct its predictions, retrain, then track a new video and export the results. Each page is one stage.

  1. Setup
  2. Create a project

  5. Correcting predictions
  6. Tracking a new video
  7. Proofreading
  8. Exporting the results
  9. What's next

Prefer to be shown?

Click Start Tutorial in the app's menu bar. It walks through steps 1–5 in the app itself, highlighting each control, and moves on once you've done each step, then runs your model on the whole training video. In the browser it stops after labeling.
